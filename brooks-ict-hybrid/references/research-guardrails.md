## Important Status

Treat those projects as `research-only`.

Do not present them as proof that a mechanical ICT signal engine has durable edge. Their strongest value is:

- analysis vocabulary
- workflow discipline
- guardrails against overclaiming
- explicit no-trade conditions

## What The Research Suggests

- Purely mechanical direction-finding from ICT-style features was not robust enough to claim durable edge.
- Named targets, session logic, PDA mapping, and trigger discipline remain useful as analysis structure.
- A checklist or audit OS is more defensible than a prophecy engine.
- Human discretion likely still carries the hardest part: deciding whether the current draw and context are real.

## How To Use This Safely

- Use the research to downgrade weak setups, not to overstate strong ones.
- Prefer scenario framing over declarations.
- Use phrases like `research-only`, `context-dependent`, and `needs confirmation`.
- If the chart is mixed, say so.

## Forbidden Jumps

- Do not say a setup is high expectancy because a local research file exists.
- Do not convert prior backtest language directly into live advice.
- Do not equate `named target exists` with `trade must work`.
